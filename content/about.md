I am a software engineer, writer and lifelong learner who enjoys understanding how people, ideas and technology come together.

My curiosity has taken me through different places, communities and disciplines. Growing up in India and later moving to the United States shaped the way I see the world. I enjoy learning from different perspectives, building connections with people and exploring ideas beyond my immediate field.

Outside of engineering, I enjoy writing, live music, traveling and conversations that challenge the way I think. These experiences continue to influence how I approach technology: with curiosity, empathy and a desire to understand the deeper problems behind the systems we build.

Professionally, I work in artificial intelligence and software engineering, focusing on building intelligent systems that move beyond experimentation into real-world applications. I am particularly interested in AI platforms, explainable AI, knowledge-driven systems and the engineering challenges involved in making AI reliable, scalable and useful.

I believe great engineering is not only about technology choices. It is about understanding the problem, making thoughtful tradeoffs and building systems that create lasting value.

My academic journey began with a master's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Patna (Class of 2015), followed by a graduate degree in Information Systems from Northeastern University (Class of 2019).

I currently live outside Boston, Massachusetts, where I continue to build, learn, write and explore how technology can help people solve meaningful problems.

This website is a place where I share my projects, ideas, experiments and lessons learned along the way.
